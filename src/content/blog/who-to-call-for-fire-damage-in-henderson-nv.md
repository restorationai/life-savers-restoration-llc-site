## Who do you call first after a house fire in Henderson?

Call 911 and the fire department first, always, even if the flames look out. Henderson Fire Department confirms your home is structurally safe to re-enter and checks for hidden hot spots in walls or attic spaces that can reignite hours later.

Once the fire department releases the scene, call your homeowners insurance company to open a claim, then call a fire damage restoration company to begin board-up, water extraction, and a soot assessment before smoke odor and firefighting water settle further into the structure.

Don't call a general contractor first. A restoration company handles the emergency mitigation, boarding windows, tarping roofs, extracting water left by hose lines, running air scrubbers, that limits how much damage there actually is for a contractor to rebuild later.

## When is it a restoration company call versus something else?

Call the fire department for anything involving active flame, smoke, or a structure you haven't been cleared to re-enter. That call always comes first, no exceptions. Call the gas or electric utility separately if first responders shut off service and you still smell gas or see exposed, damaged wiring. Call your insurance adjuster once mitigation is already underway, not before, so there's documentation to back the claim from the start.

A restoration company's job starts the moment the structure is cleared. In Henderson's stucco-and-tile-roof construction, common in master-planned neighborhoods around Green Valley and Anthem, firefighting water soaks into drywall, insulation, and subfloor in rooms the flames never reached. That water needs extraction and structural drying within the first day or two, the window described in the [IICRC's S500 water restoration standard](https://iicrc.org/s500/), or it turns into a secondary mold problem on top of the fire.

## What does a fire damage company actually do in the first 24 hours?

In the first 24 hours, a fire restoration crew secures the structure, removes standing water left by firefighting efforts, and checks how far soot and smoke traveled beyond the room that burned.

That usually means boarding broken windows and tarping roof damage so the home isn't exposed to Henderson's heat, dust, and wind overnight. Crews extract hose water and set air movers and dehumidifiers to start drying framing and subfloor before mold has a chance to take hold, which the [EPA notes can begin within 24 to 48 hours](https://www.epa.gov/mold/brief-guide-mold-moisture-and-your-home) on wet materials. They also walk the property room by room to document damage, which becomes the backbone of your insurance claim.

Soot and smoke rarely stay in the room where the fire started. Heat pulls fine soot particles through HVAC ducts and gaps around doors into rooms the flames never touched, leaving a gray film on surfaces and an odor that lingers in drywall, carpet, and fabric. That's why a fire damage scope almost always includes [smoke damage restoration](/services/smoke-damage-restoration/) for the whole structure, not just the burned room, plus a separate [odor removal](/services/odor-removal/) pass with ozone or hydroxyl treatment once visible soot is cleaned, since wiping a wall down doesn't remove odor trapped inside the materials.

Furniture, clothing, and other belongings usually need to leave the house too. [Contents restoration](/services/contents-restoration/) covers cleaning and deodorizing what can be saved and documenting what can't, which matters directly to the insurance payout. ## What should you have ready when you call?

Have your address, a rough sense of which rooms were affected, and your insurance information ready when you call. That's enough for a crew to start.

You don't need a full damage list or your policy number memorized. A restoration company that handles Henderson fire claims will walk you through documentation as part of the job, photographing and itemizing damage as they go instead of asking you to do it yourself first. If you already have a claim number, bring it, but it isn't required to get a crew moving.
