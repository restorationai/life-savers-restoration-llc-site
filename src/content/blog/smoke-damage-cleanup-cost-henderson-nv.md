Maybe a grease fire stayed contained to the stovetop and the fire department left after five minutes, but your kitchen walls now have a greasy film and the smell has drifted into the hallway. Or a wildfire several miles out sent smoke into the valley for a few days and it settled into your carpet and ductwork even though nothing in your home actually burned. Either way, you're dealing with smoke damage cleanup, not full fire restoration, and the pricing works differently than a post where framing and drywall were destroyed.

## How Much Does Smoke Damage Cleanup Cost in Henderson, NV?

Most smoke-only jobs in the Henderson area land between $300 and $4,000, depending on how much residue there is and how far it traveled.

| Scope | Typical Cost Range |

## What Drives the Price Up or Down?

The single biggest factor is whether the residue is dry and synthetic or wet and protein-based. Dry synthetic soot from burned paper, plastic, or electrical components wipes off with a dry chemical sponge in most cases. Protein residue from a grease or meat fire turns into a nearly invisible, sticky film that bonds to paint and cabinetry and needs degreasing agents and more labor time, which is why it costs two to three times as much per room.

Other things that move the number: how many rooms the smoke actually reached (smoke travels through HVAC return air far past the room of origin), whether porous materials like carpet, upholstery, or drapes absorbed odor and need replacement instead of cleaning, how long the residue sat before cleanup started, and whether the home's air handler recirculated smoke through every duct run in the house. A fast response generally keeps the job smaller because soot hasn't had time to etch into paint and finishes.

## How Are Walls, Ceilings and HVAC Ducts Cleaned After Smoke?

Walls and ceilings get cleaned with a sequence, not a single pass: dry chemical sponges lift loose soot first, then a wet degreaser handles whatever's left, then surfaces get sealed or repainted if staining remains after cleaning. Skipping the dry step and going straight to wet cleaning smears soot into the paint instead of removing it, which is a common DIY mistake.

HVAC ducts are priced separately because they require pulling registers, running a HEPA-filtered vacuum through the trunk lines, and sometimes sealing or replacing flex duct sections that absorbed odor. If the system ran during or right after the fire, assume the ducts carried soot into every room on that zone, not just the room where the fire happened. ## Why Is Odor Removal Priced as a Separate Line Item?

Odor removal is priced separately because smoke smell and smoke residue aren't the same problem. You can clean every visible surface and still have a house that smells like smoke, because odor particles work their way into drywall pores, subfloor, and HVAC insulation that a surface wipe never touches.

Thermal fogging uses a heated deodorizer fog that penetrates the same pathways smoke did, which is why it's effective but billed on its own. Hydroxyl generators run for days at a time and break down odor molecules without the evacuation requirements that ozone treatment carries, since ozone isn't safe to run in an occupied space. For a kitchen fire or wildfire smoke event, expect odor treatment to run alongside, not inside, the surface cleaning estimate. ## What Does Homeowners Insurance Usually Cover for Smoke Damage?

Most standard homeowners policies cover smoke damage from a sudden fire, whether it started in your home or a neighbor's, because fire is a named peril on nearly every policy (see the [Insurance Information Institute's overview of what a standard homeowners policy covers](https://www.iii.org/article/what-is-covered-by-standard-homeowners-insurance)). What varies is how the adjuster scopes cleaning versus replacement, especially for porous items like upholstery and drapes that may get written off rather than cleaned.

Documentation matters here. Photograph every affected surface before cleaning starts, and keep the itemized invoice broken into cleaning, odor treatment, and contents lines, since adjusters typically want to see those as separate costs rather than one lump sum. Henderson homeowners working with Life Savers Restoration LLC get that written, itemized scope up front, which is the same breakdown most insurers ask for anyway. Coverage details vary by policy and insurer, so confirm specifics with your agent before assuming a given item is covered.

## What Happens During a Smoke Damage Cleanup, Step by Step?

1. **Assessment and scope.** A technician walks the home, identifies which rooms and which HVAC zones smoke reached, and separates light residue from areas needing deeper cleaning.
2. **HVAC shutdown and containment.** The air handler gets shut off to stop recirculating soot, and affected rooms get sealed off from clean areas.
3. **Contents inventory.** Movable items get photographed, logged, and either cleaned on-site or boxed for [pack-out cleaning](/services/contents-restoration/) off-site.
4. **Surface cleaning.** Dry sponge pass first, then wet degreasing on remaining residue, working from ceiling down to floor.
5. **Odor treatment.** Thermal fogging or hydroxyl generators run once surfaces are clean, since treating odor before cleaning residue just wastes the treatment.
6. **Final walkthrough.** Surfaces and air get checked before the job closes out, and any remaining staining gets flagged for painting.
