## When do I call a plumber instead of a restoration company?

Call a plumber when the water source is still active and you cannot shut it off yourself. A restoration company removes water and dries the structure. They do not repair broken pipes, failed water heaters, or faulty supply lines. If the source is still running, a plumber stops it. Once the water is off, the restoration crew takes over.

For most Henderson homeowners, the sequence looks like this:

1. Shut off the water supply if you can reach the valve safely.
2. If you cannot shut it off, call a licensed plumber.
3. Once water is off, call Life Savers Restoration LLC at (702) 845-1325 for extraction and drying.
4. Call your insurance company after mitigation is underway, not before.

That last point matters. Calling your insurer first can slow the process. Adjusters work during business hours. Restoration crews work now. Get the water out first, document everything, then loop in your carrier.

## What happens when you call Life Savers Restoration LLC?

When you call (702) 845-1325, you reach a live person, not a voicemail. On arrival, the technician performs a moisture assessment using calibrated meters and thermal imaging to map the full extent of the damage, including areas behind walls and under flooring that look dry but are not. Henderson stucco homes with concrete slab foundations hold water differently than wood-frame construction, and the crew accounts for that.

From there, the process follows the [IICRC S500 Standard for Professional Water Damage Restoration](https://www.iicrc.org), which is the industry benchmark for drying protocols:

- **Extraction**: Truck-mounted or portable extractors pull standing water from carpet, flooring, and subfloor.
- **Drying**: Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers are placed in a calculated configuration based on the affected square footage and material types. The crew documents equipment placement and settings.
- **Monitoring**: Technicians return daily to log moisture readings. Drying is not complete until readings reach the IICRC target for the material class, typically 3 to 5 days for Category 1 (clean water) losses.
- **Documentation**: Every reading, every equipment log, every photo goes into a drying report. That report is what your adjuster needs to approve the claim.

## Does calling a restoration company affect my insurance claim?

Calling a restoration company does not hurt your claim. In most cases, it protects it. Homeowners policies generally require the policyholder to take reasonable steps to prevent further damage after a loss. Leaving water in place while waiting for an adjuster can be treated as failure to mitigate, which gives the carrier grounds to reduce or deny the claim.

Documented, professional mitigation is what adjusters want to see. A drying report with daily moisture logs, equipment records, and photos is far stronger than a homeowner's verbal account of what happened. Life Savers Restoration LLC provides that documentation as part of every job.
