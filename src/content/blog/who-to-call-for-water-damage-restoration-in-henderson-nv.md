## Who Should I Call First for Water Damage in Henderson?

If water is still flowing, your first call is to a plumber or your utility company, not a restoration company. A restoration crew cannot stop an active leak. Shut off the main water supply at the meter first (Henderson homes typically have a shutoff near the street or in the garage), then call a licensed plumber if the source isn't obvious or accessible.

If there's any chance of electrical hazard, standing water near outlets, or a gas smell, call 911 or NV Energy (1-800-962-5325) before anyone enters the space.

Once the water source is controlled and the space is safe to enter, call Life Savers Restoration LLC at (702) 845-1325. That's when restoration work can begin: extraction, drying, moisture mapping, and documentation for your insurance claim.

Here's the triage order at a glance:

1. **Active leak or burst pipe** - Shut off water at the main. Call a plumber.
2. **Electrical or gas hazard** - Leave the space. Call 911 or your utility.
3. **Water source controlled, space is safe** - Call Life Savers Restoration LLC at (702) 845-1325 for extraction and drying.
4. **Insurance claim** - Call your carrier after mitigation is underway, not before. The restoration company documents the loss; you report it.

## When Do I Call My Insurance Company?

Call your insurance company after you've contacted a restoration company, not as your first step. This surprises a lot of Henderson homeowners, but here's why it matters: your policy requires you to mitigate further damage promptly. Waiting on hold with your carrier while water soaks into the subfloor can give an adjuster grounds to reduce your payout.

Life Savers Restoration LLC works directly with insurance carriers and handles the documentation that protects your claim: moisture readings, photo logs, drying records, and a written scope of work. Their crews are trained on the [IICRC S500 Standard for Professional Water Damage Restoration](https://www.iicrc.org), the industry benchmark adjusters reference when reviewing claims.

What your homeowners policy typically covers for sudden, accidental water damage:
- Burst pipes
- Appliance failures (washing machine hose, water heater)
- Roof leaks from a sudden storm event

What it typically excludes:
- Gradual leaks (a slow drip behind a wall that went unnoticed for months)
- Flooding from outside the home (that requires a separate flood policy through the [National Flood Insurance Program](https://www.fema.gov/flood-insurance))
- Sewer backup (often available as an add-on rider)

## What Happens When You Call Life Savers Restoration LLC?

When you call (702) 845-1325, you reach a live person, not an answering service. Here's what the first call and arrival typically look like.

**On the phone:** Be ready to describe where the water is coming from (or was coming from), which rooms are affected, and whether the source has been stopped. The team will ask about flooring type, any visible standing water, and whether you've already contacted your insurer. This takes about five minutes.

**On arrival:** The crew conducts a moisture assessment using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters. They map the affected area, classify the water (Category 1 clean water, Category 2 gray water, or Category 3 black water such as sewage), and determine the class of loss based on how much material has absorbed moisture.

This classification matters. A Category 1, Class 1 loss (a small area of clean water, hard flooring) dries differently and costs less than a Category 2, Class 3 loss (gray water that has wicked into walls and subfloor). The written scope reflects the actual conditions, not a one-size estimate.

**Extraction and drying setup:** Truck-mounted extractors pull standing water fast. Then air movers and LGR dehumidifiers are positioned according to the drying plan. In Henderson's dry desert climate, ambient conditions can help, but the [EPA notes](https://www.epa.gov/mold/mold-cleanup-your-home) that materials must reach dry standard within 24-48 hours to prevent mold growth, so equipment placement is precise, not approximate.

**Monitoring:** Crews return daily to check moisture readings and adjust equipment. Drying typically takes 3-5 days for a standard loss. You receive a drying log, which your adjuster will want to see.

If affected materials can't be dried in place (saturated drywall, compromised flooring), the crew handles controlled demolition and can coordinate [contents restoration](/services/contents-restoration/) for salvageable belongings.

## What If There's Mold Already?

Henderson's dry climate is not mold-proof. Homes in Green Valley, Anthem, and the older neighborhoods near Water Street can develop mold within 24-72 hours of a water event if drying doesn't start quickly. If you're seeing discoloration, smelling a musty odor, or dealing with a loss that sat for more than a day before extraction started, mention it on the call.

Life Savers Restoration LLC holds IICRC AMRT (Applied Microbial Remediation Technician) certification, so if mold is found during the water damage assessment, they can address it without bringing in a separate contractor. You don't need to have everything organized before you pick up the phone. But having these details speeds up the first conversation:

- Address and which rooms are affected
- Source of the water (if known) and whether it's been stopped

- Whether anyone with health sensitivities (respiratory conditions, young children) is in the home
